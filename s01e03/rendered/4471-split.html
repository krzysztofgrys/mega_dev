How the reports were split

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports across pages. Counts use unique ids.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (its locale and booking form, locales.ts) and the channel, not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong.
  • The one-day moves are the reports the desk itself noted in desk-notes.json. The desk files them by tenant, so they stay in the parser count and show in the footnote. They are a third bug all the same: the parser fix does not close them.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line is not the same bug. Read the cause, then merge.
  • A footnote under a count is a sign the count is wrong.
